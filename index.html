PERSONAL ACADEMIC HOMEPAGE

About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at Anhui University. My research centers on unaligned RGB-T tracking and pedestrian attribute recognition (PAR). I study how to connect visual information across sensors and how to recognize people from rich semantic attributes.

In RGB-T tracking, I work on cross-modal alignment, fusion, and robust tracking when visible and thermal images do not line up. In PAR, I explore vision-language learning, structured prediction, and cross-domain benchmarks.
